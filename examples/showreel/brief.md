# MOTION GOD MODE / proof film

**Mode:** showreel  
**Subject:** Code-directed motion design  
**Viewer:** Developer/creative who wants a finished film rather than AI slideshow  
**End state:** Remember the word MOTION GOD MODE and know what it stands for  
**Length:** 8 seconds  
**Size / FPS:** 960 × 540 / 24 fps (small proof video, not delivery master)  
**Brand:** Original MOTION GOD MODE demo identity  
**Facts:** None; original artistic statements only  
**Assets:** Vectors, type rendered from locally installed open system font; score synthesised by script  
**Sound:** Original generated score  
**Toolchain:** Pillow + FFmpeg; each frame generated from integer time  
**Autonomy:** unattended  
**Signoff:** MOTION GOD MODE / NO CAMERA. NO SLOP. JUST CINEMA, COMPILED.

## Decisions for blank brief lines

- 24 fps: intentionally editorial frame cadence.
- 16:9: fits repo hero preview.
- 8 seconds: low-cost reproducible technical demonstration.
- No third-party visuals or brand claims: demonstrates code ownership without unsupported claims.

## Three treatments considered

1. **Continuity bar (selected):** One bright bar progressively becomes an underline, a ring, and finally a branded block. Strong identity, low technical risk, and very legible on mobile.
2. **Mechanical typesetter:** A line of type becomes a physical press. Rejected because a 3D workflow adds dependencies without benefiting this proof.
3. **Fake operating system:** A UI evolves into a film. Rejected because a simulated product could be mistaken for a real one.

The preview proves renderer mechanics; it is not a claim that all generated films will match this visual quality.
